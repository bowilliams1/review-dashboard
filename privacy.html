BOSH Chemical — Privacy Notice

Ads Optimizer & Review Command Center — internal reporting tools

This page describes how BOSH Chemical's internal software tools use data obtained through Amazon's Login with Amazon and Advertising API.

Purpose

These are private, internal tools built for BOSH Chemical's own use. They connect to Amazon's Advertising API solely to read BOSH Chemical's own advertising campaign performance data (spend, clicks, keywords, and sales figures) for internal reporting and analysis.

Data collected

Only aggregate advertising performance data belonging to BOSH Chemical's own Amazon Advertising account. These tools do not collect, store, or share any personal information about Amazon customers or any third party.

Data sharing

Data is never sold or shared with any third party. It is stored only in BOSH Chemical's private Google Sheets and Google Apps Script, and used solely to generate internal summary reports for company use.

No automated actions

These tools are read-only with respect to Amazon Advertising — they never place, modify, or cancel any advertising campaign, bid, or budget automatically. All recommendations are reviewed and acted on by a human.
